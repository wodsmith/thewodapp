Game Day privacy

About Game Day

WODsmith Game Day is an iPhone companion for WODsmith competitions. Spectators can browse public competitions without signing in. Athletes sign in to view their own registration, schedule, and competition announcements.

Information used by the app

When you sign in, your email and password are transmitted to WODsmith over HTTPS for authentication. WODsmith stores account information, including your name, email, and user identifier. The app can update your profile name. Registration, team, check-in, heat assignment, and leaderboard information are supplied by WODsmith and your competition organizer.

On your iPhone

Your session credential is stored in the iOS Keychain and is not synchronized to other devices. Downloaded profile, registration, competition, and schedule information is cached on your device for reading during connection loss. Signing out clears this athlete cache and scheduled reminders. iOS may also clear temporary cached data.

Notifications and Live Activities

Heat reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Reminder preferences stay on your device. A Live Activity displays the competition, workout, heat, lane, venue, and countdown you choose on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. Anyone able to view your Lock Screen may see these details. You can end the countdown from Profile. Reopen the app to receive organizer schedule changes.

Tracking and sharing

The native app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Requests to WODsmith are processed by its hosting infrastructure. WODsmith uses operational logs and server monitoring, including Sentry and PostHog, to diagnose errors and slow requests. These records can include request paths, status, duration, and account identifiers when available. They support reliability and security, not advertising or cross-company tracking. We require service providers that process user data on our behalf to protect it to the same or an equivalent standard described in this policy and required by Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines, and to use it only to provide their services to WODsmith. Competition information is shared according to the organizer’s publication settings. Opening WODsmith pages uses the website’s privacy practices.

Retention and your choices

Device caches are retained until sign-out or removal by iOS. Your WODsmith account and registration records remain on the server according to the full WODsmith privacy policy. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact privacy@wodsmith.com. Do not include your password. Public competition results and records required for legitimate operational or legal purposes may be retained as described in that policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@wodsmith.com. This policy supplements the full WODsmith privacy policy at https://wodsmith.com/privacy. Updated September 5, 2026.

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

ios

## Users

Athletes checking heat times, lane assignments, workouts, and registration information at a competition. Spectators browse competitions, schedules, workouts, public announcements, and standings without signing in.

## Product Purpose

Make the athlete’s next start and the rest of their schedule easy to check on an iPhone. WODsmith remains the server and source of organizer-published information.

## Operating Context

Often one-handed, interrupted, outdoors or in a bright venue, with unreliable connectivity. An athlete needs an unambiguous competition-local date, start time, lane, venue, and honest download freshness.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The user explicitly requested native Swift, a home page with registered competitions followed by the competition directory, an athlete-first competition detail page, configurable heat reminders, a Lock Screen countdown, and App Store distribution. Local reminders require downloaded assignments; remote APNs schedule synchronization is not implemented.

## Brand Commitments

WODsmith’s anvil mark and orange identity. Native iOS navigation, San Francisco text styles, adaptive system surfaces, and readable contrast take precedence over transferring web marketing treatments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use Dynamic Type, 44-point interactive targets, native labeled controls, content-driven layouts, explicit dates, high-contrast text in both appearances, and accessible progress/error feedback. iPhone is the declared device family.

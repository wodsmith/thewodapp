# Crew volunteer import

Operate-mode surface for organizers who need to import a volunteer roster from CSV or Excel without losing track of when data becomes persistent.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One visible worksheet carries the entire import; it refuses modal compression and surprise actions.

OWN-WORLD: Crew's neutral cards, thin rules, compact workhorse type, and orange action color distinguish current, ready, waiting, error, and complete states.

STORY: The organizer chooses and maps a file, reviews exact row outcomes, then deliberately imports only ready volunteers.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Page purpose and safety promises lead into a compact three-stage rail; the file control begins immediately below, with preview and import actions visible in their owning sections.

FORM: Persistent three-stage operator worksheet, assigned structure 6, seed `ff3d5a27`.

FINISH: Unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with a finish-review verdict and this durable surface brief.

## Constraints

Preserve the existing preview and apply APIs, keep preview read-only, keep blocked rows out of apply, and adapt to a single mobile column without hiding stages or primary actions.
